# The emulator proof, 2026-10-14

The app at `b72b2d2` (debug build, `com.palli.mobile.debug`, 437 unit tests green) run on the `deflock34` AVD (Android 14, x86_64, KVM-accelerated, headless) against an isolated proof backend on `127.0.0.1:8100` (the emulator reaches it as `http://10.0.2.2:8100`). The proof backend runs on a copy of the data with the background engines off. The live PaLLi on :8000/:3000 was never touched.

Model turns: **5 messages reached her, as 4 model turns.** The two texts typed offline were sent back to back on reconnect and her owner lane merged them into one turn. The others were the inline Reply, the photo and the voice note. The agreed cap was 5, and no retry was needed.

Driving: `scripts/ui.py` (tap, hold and `has` by exact label; `labels` lists what is on screen), `adb`, and read-only `sqlite3` on the proof database. No token value appears in this file, the screenshots or the logs.

**Step 3: boot, smoke test, install.**

- Happened: boot completed on the first poll; the smoke test ran 1 test with 0 failures; the app installed and opened on "Pair with PaLLi". Screenshot: none (the Pair screen is in `13-after-unpair.png`).

**Check A: link while unpaired, Cancel.**

- Expected: dialog "Pair with <address>?", then back on Pair, and no new device.

**Step 4: pair through the link.**
- Did: fired the same link again (the code was not used up by the Cancel), tapped **Pair**.

- Note: events belong to the owner, not to one phone, so a newly paired phone replays the whole timeline, including what the curl device sent. That is by design (`mobile_events` is keyed by `owner_id`).

**Step 8: Android kills the app.**
- Did: Home, then `am kill`, then (see below) a real kill, then watched.
- Expected: the service is back (START_STICKY).
- Happened: `am kill` is a no-op here. The process holds a foreground service, so Android does not treat it as a background process: the pid stayed 8108 for 20 s and "service back" printed only because the service had never left. That makes the brief's check pass for the wrong reason. A real kill, `adb shell run-as com.palli.mobile.debug kill -9 <pid>`, ended pid 8108; Android restarted the process as pid 8680 within about 2 s, the connection service was foreground again (`startRequested=true`), the shade said "Connected to PaLLi", the device's "last seen" moved to 16:19, and the app opened on chat with "connected". Screenshot: none.

**Step 9: unpair from the backend.**
- Did: `phone revoke 2` with the app in the background, then opened the app.
- Expected: Pair with "This phone was unpaired."
- Happened: the revoke was at 16:20:24; the app showed "This phone was unpaired." on the Pair screen about 8 s after it was opened, and the connection service was gone. `phone list` no longer lists the device. Screenshot: `11-unpaired.png`.

**Check G: app-side unpair.**
- Did: paired again with a fresh code (device 3, no model turn), listed the app's files, opened Settings, Unpair this phone, **Cancel**, then Unpair this phone and **Unpair**.
- Expected: a confirm step; then the Pair screen, the device gone from `phone list`, and nothing of hers left in `cache/`.
- Happened: the dialog read "Unpair this phone?" ("...The conversation and files on this phone are cleared; they stay on PaLLi's machine."). Cancel left the app on Settings with the device still listed. Unpair went to the Pair screen, with no "was unpaired" warning (it was the owner's own choice). `phone list` no longer lists device 3 and the connection service stopped. Before the unpair `cache/image_cache` held two entries (the macaw, about 2.2 MB); after it, only the `journal` file. Screenshots: `12-unpair-confirm.png`, `13-after-unpair.png`.
